How Scam Shield decides

Every answer comes from rules you can read below. AI, when switched on, adds reasoning and reads screenshots, but it can only raise a warning, never lower one.

  1. ReadThe message text, or the fields in the screenshot (by AI or typed by you).
  2. MatchKnown scam patterns in Bangla, English and Banglish; links and phone numbers are checked separately.
  3. CompareFor payments: amount, your number, time, TrxID, editing-app traces and reused screenshots.
  4. ExplainYou see which words or checks caused the warning, and what to do next.

Privacy

We never ask for your PIN or OTP. Checks are not stored on our server; your recent checks stay in this browser only. With AI on, the text or image is sent to the AI provider to be read.

What it cannot do

  • It cannot prove a payment is real. Only your own account can.
  • New scam wording may not match any pattern yet.
  • Spotting edited images is a clue, not forensic proof.
